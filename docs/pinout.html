Wire colours

The same signal is the same colour at both ends of its wire. Red is always 3V3, black is always GND, orange is always 5 V. A colour can repeat when the wires never sit side by side, and inside a piece the pin labels settle which is which.

Wiring order: ground and power first. Check every ground and the 5 V rail with a multimeter, with nothing plugged in, before connecting a device. Then the Teensy side of each port, then each outer piece one at a time. The full checklist is in docs/wiring-checklist.md.
