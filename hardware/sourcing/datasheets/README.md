# Datasheets

Manufacturer datasheets for the parts in [parts.md](../parts.md), saved on 2026-10-21 from the links Digi-Key lists for each part
(Texas Instruments' came from its public PDF address). They belong to their manufacturers and are kept here for reference only.
Each part's `Datasheet` column in `parts.md` links to its copy, or to the maker's page when no copy is saved.

Not saved, because the maker's site refused an automated download or the link is not a PDF: the Würth fuse holder and 47 µF capacitor,
the Littelfuse fuse, the Stackpole resistors, the DFRobot DFR0669 (a wiki page), and the Teensy 4.1 and fader nubbin (no datasheet link).
Several parts share one file (the three resistors, the two screw terminals).
